History Of Internet in Singapore

Singapore’s very first internet journey began in the late 1980s within academia. In 1987, NUS connected to BITNET, linking local researchers with universities abroad. By 1990, NUS had established the country’s first internet connection, marking Singapore’s entry into the digital age. A year later, with support from the National Science and Technology Board, NUS launched Technet—Singapore’s first research network—opening the door to wider connectivity and laying the foundation for the internet we know today.
